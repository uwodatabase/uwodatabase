Aboardage Easier to engage in Melee Battle.
Accuracy Shots hit the enemy easier and can also be fired at point blank range.
Arms Thanks to good knowledge of weapons, you are able to operate powerful equipment on special ships.
Assault Temporary increase in Attack Power during Melee Battle.
Ballistics Extends cannon firing range.
Evasion Makes it difficult for enemy bombs to strike during battle.
Fire Fighting Extinguish fire at sea.
First Aid Lowers the loss of crew due to injury during battle or exploration.
Guard Temporary increase in Defence Power during Melee Battle.
Gunfire Decreases the Attack Power/Defence Power of the enemy during Melee Battle. Attack speed of guns increases in Land Battle.
Gunnery Cannon loads become quicker and the cannon less prone to failure.
Leadership Alleviation of issues with the crew such as disorder, fighting, and mutiny.
Lookout You are able to know the name of the captain of a far off ship.
Mine laying Ability to lay sea mines during battle.
Pathology Medical treatment for outbreaks of scurvy or infectious disease at sea.
Penetration Increases destructive capability of cannon.
Plunder After victory in hand to hand combat or a land battle, plunder your enemy's possessions.
Reloading Temporary increase in speed of cannon loads.
Repair Timber can be used to repair ships.
Request Convoy Support from your country's navy is called. Cannot be used for personal disputes.
Request reinforce Call for reinforcements from another fleet.
Rowing Increases speed of vessels fitted with oars.
Shipbuilding Ships can be built at the Shipyard.
Sniping Increases the attack speed of guns and bows in land battle.
Steering Increases ship Turning Speed. Also lessens damage from sea mines, etc.
Surgery Medical treatment for crew injured in battle. Water is required.
Sword Mastery The attack power and attack speed of close-range weapons in land battles are raised.
Swordplay The attack power in melee battles and the attack power of close-range weapons in land battles is raised.
Tactics Can increase Attack and Defence Power during Melee Battle.
